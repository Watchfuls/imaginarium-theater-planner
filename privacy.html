Privacy Policy

Imaginarium Theater Planner · Last updated 3 November 2026

This unofficial fan-made planner is maintained by Watchful. You can use its planning features without an account. Settings are saved in your browser; Google Drive backups and feedback are optional.

Browser saves and backup files

The planner uses browser local storage to remember your owned characters and levels, challenge setup, selected cast, act teams, encounter choices, historical plans, and sort preference. This information stays on that browser unless you choose to export it, save it to Google Drive, or include it in feedback.

Inventory JSON imports are processed in your browser. The planner extracts character ownership and levels; it does not upload your original inventory file. Downloaded backups contain player settings and plans, not your full inventory. Restoring a backup reads it in your browser.

Browser saves remain until you clear this site's storage, your browser removes it, or you replace the data. To remove them, close the planner and clear its site data in your browser settings. Delete downloaded backup files separately from your devices or any storage you copied them to.

Optional Google Drive backups

When you connect Google Drive, Google asks you to authorize access to the planner's private application-data folder using drive.appdata. This does not grant the planner access to your other Drive documents. The planner does not request your email address, profile, contacts, or Google password.

  • Save to Drive sends the same compact player backup described above directly from your browser to your Google Drive account.
  • Load from Drive reads that backup and its file information, such as its identifier and modification time, to restore your settings.
  • Transfers are manual. Changes save locally in the browser, but are not automatically synchronized with Drive.

Your Google access token is held temporarily in browser memory. It is not included in backups or saved in local storage. Disconnecting forgets that connection on this device; it does not delete your Drive backup or revoke Google's authorization.

The planner has no operator-run server receiving copies of your Drive backups. Google user data is used only to provide the backup and restore features you request. It is not sold, used for advertising, or sent to Tally or YouTube by the planner. Our handling of Google API data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

The cloud backup remains until you replace it or delete it through Google Drive's app-management controls. To remove it, open Drive settings, choose Manage apps, find the planner, and use the option to delete hidden app data where available. You can revoke access separately through your Google Account connections. See Google's storage management guidance and Google's Privacy Policy.

Optional feedback through Tally

Opening Send feedback loads an embedded Tally form and its supporting script. Tally receives connection information when the form loads and processes the information you provide. The form may offer a message, feedback category, season or act, reference links, file uploads, and optional contact details.

Submitting feedback shares those answers and any uploaded files with Tally and the planner maintainer so the report can be reviewed, issues investigated, and replies sent if you provide contact details. The planner does not automatically attach your roster, backup, or Google token. Avoid including passwords or other sensitive information.

Feedback may be retained while reviewing reports and maintaining the planner. For a request to access, correct, or delete a response, contact Watchful through the feedback form and provide enough detail to identify it. Anonymous responses may be difficult to identify. Tally may use cookies or browser storage, including to retain form progress according to its settings. Read Tally's Privacy Notice.

YouTube videos and other links

The Video Guide tab loads a YouTube player using youtube-nocookie.com. Loading or interacting with the player sends requests to Google/YouTube and may involve browser storage or cookies under their policies. This mode does not make the player anonymous. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Links to other tools, enemy wiki pages, and external websites open services with their own privacy practices. Those services receive information when you visit them.

Hosting and third-party services

The published planner is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as described in its Pages documentation and GitHub's Privacy Statement.

The planner does not add its own advertising or analytics tracking. Google sign-in loads when you open Saves & backups with Drive configured; Tally loads when you open feedback; YouTube loads when you open Video Guide. These providers handle their own service data under their policies.

Questions and updates

For privacy questions or requests about information you shared with the maintainer, use the feedback form and label your message “Privacy request.” This policy will be updated if the planner's data handling changes; the date above identifies the latest revision.
