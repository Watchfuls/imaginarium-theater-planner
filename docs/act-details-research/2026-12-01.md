# December 2026 season lineup

The user identified the Version 7.2 patch notes as the source for the December
lineup: https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Version/7.2. The patch-note
lineup is official information: featured elements Pyro, Hydro, and Geo;
opening characters Arlecchino, Bennett, Furina, Xingqiu, Navia, and Illuga;
special guests Chasca, Varesa, Kinich, and Iansan. The opening lineup receives
Fantastical Blessings while the theater is open.

Season 30 and the December-to-January timestamps follow the existing monthly
reset convention. The announced lineup is stored in
`src/data/theater-lineup-overrides.json` so the Fandom refresh preserves it.

No battle-detail claims are made in this official lineup record. Keep any
unverified encounter preparation outside tracked and deployed data until it is
officially published and checked.
